\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

The quantitative runs are queued in the overnight compute window (Section~\ref{sec:repro}); at the time of
this build the GPU was in use by the owner's own work, so no run had started. As each run finishes,
\texttt{jigbench report} regenerates its table and figure from the trial records and they appear here
automatically. All such results will be labelled \pilot{} with Wilson 95\% intervals until the full,
multi-seed runs complete; pilots carry no hypothesis tests.
