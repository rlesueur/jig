\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Every work cited here was retrieved from the arXiv API and its title checked against our bibliography
entry; summaries come from the abstracts unless stated. A general web search was not available when the
review was made, so work outside arXiv is under-represented.

\paragraph{Long-term memory.} MemGPT pages information between the context window and slower
stores~\citep{packer2023memgpt}; Generative Agents retrieve by recency, importance and relevance and
periodically reflect into higher-level memories~\citep{park2023generative}; MemoryBank adds a forgetting
curve~\citep{zhong2023memorybank}; A-MEM links agent-written notes~\citep{xu2025amem}; Mem0 extracts and
consolidates salient facts~\citep{chhikara2025mem0}; Zep keeps a temporal knowledge
graph~\citep{rasmussen2025zep}. CoALA supplies the vocabulary of memory types~\citep{sumers2023coala}, and
long contexts are used unevenly~\citep{liu2023lost}. Benchmarks include LoCoMo~\citep{maharana2024locomo},
LongMemEval with knowledge-update and abstention questions~\citep{wu2024longmemeval}, MemoryArena, where
memory must guide later actions~\citep{he2026memoryarena}, Memora with a forgetting-aware
score~\citep{uddin2026memora}, and LongMemEval-V2~\citep{wu2026longmemevalv2}.

\paragraph{Compaction as a safety surface.} Constraints obeyed while visible are removed by
compaction~\citep{chen2026governancedecay}; compactors keep a minority of session
constraints~\citep{wang2026lostincompaction}; rewriting turns hedges into confident
facts~\citep{kwon2026manufactured}; and persisted authority labels cut unauthorised actions to zero in
AuthMem-Bench~\citep{zhan2026authmem}. Consolidation itself should be measured and
governed~\citep{annapureddy2026epistemics}.

\paragraph{Indirect injection and exfiltration.} Indirect injection was defined by Greshake et
al.~\citep{greshake2023indirect} and formalised by Liu et al.~\citep{liu2023formalizing}; benchmarks
include BIPIA~\citep{yi2023bipia}, InjecAgent~\citep{zhan2024injecagent},
AgentDojo~\citep{debenedetti2024agentdojo}, Agent Security Bench~\citep{zhang2024asb},
WASP~\citep{evtimov2025wasp} and, for malicious users, AgentHarm~\citep{andriushchenko2024agentharm}.
ToolEmu emulates tools with a language model~\citep{ruan2023toolemu}; we use real local services
instead. Silent Egress reports that a malicious page induces exfiltrating requests from a local 7B agent
most of the time and that output-based checks miss most successes~\citep{lan2026silentegress}. Adaptive
attacks break most in-band defences~\citep{narisetty2026adaptive}, so static results such as ours are an
upper bound on robustness.

\paragraph{Memory poisoning.} Early attacks assumed write access to the
store~\citep{zou2024poisonedrag,chen2024agentpoison}; MINJA injects through queries
alone~\citep{dong2025minja}, and MemoryGraft plants fake successful experiences~\citep{srivastava2025memorygraft}.
In 2026, persistence became the focus: eTAMP~\citep{zou2026etamp}, sleeper
poisoning~\citep{pulipaka2026sleeper}, cross-session stored injection~\citep{xie2026crosssession},
MPBench~\citep{dash2026mpbench}, WhisperBench~\citep{zhang2026whisperbench}, Bad
Memory~\citep{gadgil2026badmemory}, MemSecBench~\citep{chen2026memsecbench} and
PMPA~\citep{huang2026pmpa}. A survey argues for storage-time provenance and
retention~\citep{lin2026ltmsurvey}, and MemGate gates retrieval~\citep{zhang2026memgate}.

\paragraph{Guards, monitors and system defences.} Guard classifiers include Llama
Guard~\citep{inan2023llamaguard}, ShieldGemma~\citep{zeng2024shieldgemma} and Granite
Guardian~\citep{padhi2024graniteguardian}; GuardAgent reviews agent actions~\citep{xiang2024guardagent};
LlamaFirewall combines several layers~\citep{chennabasappa2025llamafirewall}. System designs separate
trusted control from untrusted data: the dual-LLM pattern~\citep{willison2023dualllm},
CaMeL~\citep{debenedetti2025camel} and the design patterns of Beurer-Kellner et
al.~\citep{beurerkellner2025patterns}; spotlighting~\citep{hines2024spotlighting} and instruction
hierarchies~\citep{wallace2024hierarchy} work inside the model. Weaker monitors can supervise stronger
agents with the right scaffolding~\citep{kale2025weaktostrong}, and PACT studies per-call
trust~\citep{fan2026pact}. Human approvals habituate over time~\citep{yu2026habituation}, and approvals
must bind to the exact executed call~\citep{kumar2026loopjacking}.

\paragraph{Quantisation and proactive agents.} Quantisation can degrade safety
alignment~\citep{kharinaev2025quant}, and standard 4-bit quantisation is mostly safety-neutral but
unstable at high sampling temperature~\citep{prasad2026quanttemp}; neither covers ternary models or
agentic injection. Proactive agents act on observed activity~\citep{lu2024proactive,oh2026proactivefoundations},
building on the ReAct loop~\citep{yao2022react}.

\paragraph{Gap.} We found no evaluation of an isolated, intent-only action reviewer against
memory-mediated attacks; no comparison of reviewer choice against approval burden on one consumer GPU;
and no agentic injection or memory study of a ternary model against a standard 4-bit quantisation of
the same base model.
