\section{Ethics}
\label{sec:ethics}

The memory study uses LongMemEval under the MIT licence; its conversations are constructed benchmark
data, not personal data. No attack content is authored in this programme: the planned safety
evaluation runs published benchmarks' existing test cases, under their licences, against local models
only. LoCoMo and BIPIA declare no licence through their repository metadata, so we do not redistribute
their data. No human participants were involved; a study of real owners' approval behaviour is future
work.
