\subsection{C1 pilot: memory strategies}
\label{sec:results-c1}

The C1 pilot replays four LongMemEval-S questions (one each of knowledge update, temporal reasoning,
multi-session and single-session user), four sessions per question, through each of the five strategies,
with the ternary Bonsai 2 27B agent and granite 4.2 8B as judge (the full run uses Gemma 4 26B-A4B as
judge). Nineteen of the twenty planned trials finished; the remaining one (rolling summary on the
knowledge-update question) stopped because the agent's summary rewrite reached the 8192-token output
limit, and is reported as a harness error rather than scored (Table~\ref{tab:c1-c1-pilot-2026-10-01}).

Retrieval answered all four questions (4/4; Wilson 95\% interval 51--100\%) at about 10{,}100 prompt
tokens per question. Both consolidation strategies answered three of four (75\%; 30--95\%) but spent
three to four times as many prompt tokens (about 44{,}500 keeping history, 35{,}000 forgetting), because
every session triggers an extraction task and every third a consolidation task. The rolling summary
answered two of three (67\%; 21--94\%) and bounded full context one of four (25\%; 5--70\%), both at about
7{,}400--8{,}100 tokens. Every strategy except retrieval missed the multi-session question. With $n \le 4$ per cell every interval overlaps, so these are \pilot{} observations,
not findings, and carry no hypothesis tests.

\paragraph{Finding: reasoning models spend their output budget counting words.} In the full run, every
rolling-summary attempt so far was cut off at the 8{,}192-token output limit with no visible output: 5 of
5 attempts on 3 questions. Diagnostic replays (streamed and not scored) of the three latest attempts
recorded 27{,}345, 28{,}403 and 29{,}384 characters of hidden reasoning, in which the model counted the
summary's words one by one to keep it ``under 400 words''. The reasoning was not a loop: 89\%, 100\% and
98\% of its lines were distinct. The instruction was cheap to write but expensive to obey. From protocol
v0.4 the prompt drops the limit and leaves everything else unchanged. All 30 rolling-summary trials re-run
as their own run, and the cut-off attempts stay in the earlier run as this finding; neither is pooled with
the other.
